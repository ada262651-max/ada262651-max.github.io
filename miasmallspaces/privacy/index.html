Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Mia Small Spaces (“this site”) collects when you visit, and what it does not. The short version: this is a simple static website with no accounts, no comments, no forms, and no analytics — so it collects almost nothing.

Information this site collects

This site does not ask for, store, or process personal information. There is no sign-up, no newsletter form, no comment section, and no contact form on this site, and this site does not run advertising or analytics scripts.

Like almost every website, this site is delivered by a hosting provider (GitHub Pages), which may automatically record standard technical information in server logs — such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested — for security and operational purposes. That logging is handled by the hosting provider under its own privacy policy, and I do not access or use those logs to identify visitors.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own. If that ever changes (for example, if analytics are added in the future), this policy will be updated first.

Links to other websites

This site links to Pinterest and may link to retailers and other third-party websites, including through affiliate links (see the Affiliate Disclosure). When you click a link and leave this site, the destination site’s own privacy policy applies. Third parties, including affiliate programs and retailers, may use cookies to track a purchase made through an affiliate link so that a commission can be credited. I do not control those cookies and do not receive personal information about you from them.

Children’s privacy

This site is written for adults organizing their homes and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

Because this site does not collect personal information, there is no account to delete and no data to export. For anything related to Pinterest or a retailer, please use their respective privacy controls.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Contact page.
