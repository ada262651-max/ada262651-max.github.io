Hi, I’m Mia Carter.

I live in a small apartment — the kind where the kitchen counter doubles as a desk, the “pantry” is one heroic cabinet, and every closet is doing the job of three. For a long time I treated that as a temporary problem. Then I realized something: small isn’t the problem. Unplanned is the problem.

Mia Small Spaces is where I share what actually works in a home like mine: kitchen & pantry organization that doubles your storage without doubling your square footage, tiny apartment storage solutions that use the dead zones you already have, and renter-friendly upgrades that come down clean when it’s time to move.

  • Small kitchen & pantry organization — systems for one-cabinet kitchens and no-pantry apartments.
  • Tiny apartment storage — room-by-room ideas for studios and one-bedrooms.
  • Bathroom & entryway fixes — the two smallest, hardest-working spots in any home.
  • Budget home finds — practical, affordable tools and organizers, tested against real small spaces.
  • Rental-friendly everything — if it needs a drill or risks the deposit, I’ll tell you up front and offer the no-drill version.

My rules for a small home

  • Closed storage beats open storage. Doors and bins hide visual noise; small rooms need calm more than they need display shelves.
  • Vertical is free square footage. Walls, doors, and the space above cabinets are storage you’re already paying rent on.
  • Every item gets an address. If it doesn’t have a home, it becomes clutter — no matter how small the apartment is.
  • One in, one out. In a small space, this isn’t minimalism. It’s physics.
