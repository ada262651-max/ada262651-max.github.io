9 Space-Saving Kitchen Gadgets for Tiny Kitchens That Actually Work

By Mia Carter · November 2026 · Space-Saving Kitchen Gadgets

Living in a small apartment taught me one thing quickly: in a tiny kitchen, the gadget is only good if it has a home when it is not in use. Everything on this list stacks, hangs, nests or folds, so it earns its footprint instead of stealing counter space. These are the space-saving kitchen gadgets I recommend to anyone setting up a small kitchen from scratch.

1. Stackable shelf risers

A shelf riser turns one cabinet shelf into two. I use them for bowls and mugs — the two things that somehow multiply. Measure your cabinet height first; most risers add about 5–6 inches of usable space.

2. Over-the-sink dish rack

If your counter has no room for a drying rack, one that spans the sink uses space that is otherwise wasted. Roll-up versions disappear into a drawer when you are done.

3. Slim rolling cart

The 6-inch gap next to a fridge or stove is dead space in most rentals. A slim rolling cart turns it into pantry storage for oils, spices and cleaning supplies.

4. Magnetic spice tins

Spice jars eat cabinet space. Magnetic tins stick to the side of the fridge and keep labels visible, which also means fewer duplicate purchases.

5. Nesting bowls and measuring sets

Anything that nests is a win. One set of nesting bowls replaces a cabinet full of mismatched plastic.

6. Over-the-door pantry rack

Renters cannot always add shelves. An over-the-door rack adds five or six rows of storage to a pantry or closet door with no drilling and no damage.

7. Collapsible colander and dish tub

Collapsible silicone pieces flatten to about an inch thick and slide next to cutting boards. Small change, daily difference.

8. Under-shelf hanging baskets

These clip onto an existing shelf and hang below it — ideal for wraps, lids and the small packets that get lost at the back of drawers.

9. Drawer dividers you can adjust

Spring-loaded dividers keep utensils sorted without a bulky tray. They adjust to the drawer you actually have, which matters in older apartments.

How to choose

Start with the one spot that frustrates you every day — for most small kitchens that is the sink area or one overstuffed cabinet. Fix that one spot first, live with it for a week, then decide what is still missing. Buying all nine at once usually just moves the clutter around.
