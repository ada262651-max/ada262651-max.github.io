Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: November 12, 2026

In the interest of full transparency — and in accordance with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines on endorsements and disclosures — this page explains how Mia Small Spaces earns money.

Some links are affiliate links

Some of the links on this website, and in my Pinterest pins, are affiliate links. That means if you click one of those links and make a purchase, I may earn a small commission from the retailer or affiliate program, at no additional cost to you. The price you pay is exactly the same as if you had gone to the retailer directly.

How it works

  • You click a product or resource link on this site or in one of my pins.
  • The retailer records that you arrived from Mia Small Spaces, usually with a cookie that lasts for a limited time.
  • If you make a qualifying purchase, a small percentage of the sale is paid to me as a commission.
  • Commissions help cover the cost of running this site and creating new content. They never change what you pay.

My promise to you

  • I only recommend products and ideas that fit the small-space philosophy of this site — practical, renter-conscious, and worth their footprint.
  • A commission never buys a recommendation. If something doesn’t work in a small home, it doesn’t get featured, affiliate program or not.
  • Content comes first. Most of what I publish is purely informational. Affiliate links appear only where a specific product genuinely helps carry out an idea.

A note on prices

Product prices and availability change frequently and without notice. Any price or availability shown by a retailer belongs to that retailer’s page at the moment you view it — please check there for the current details before purchasing.

Questions

If anything about how this site earns money is unclear, please reach out through the Contact page. I’m happy to explain.

Thank you for supporting Mia Small Spaces by reading, saving, and sharing — it genuinely helps keep the ideas coming.
